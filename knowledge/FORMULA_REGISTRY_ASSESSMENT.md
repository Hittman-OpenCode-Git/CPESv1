## Purpose

A formula registry catalogs every formula, equation, and quantitative relationship tested across CMA Part 1. Each entry specifies:
- Formula name and standard notation
- Variables and their definitions
- Applicable topic domain (A.1–A.6, B.1–B.4, etc.)
- Calculation context (direct computation vs. variance analysis vs. ratio interpretation)
- Question IDs that reference this formula

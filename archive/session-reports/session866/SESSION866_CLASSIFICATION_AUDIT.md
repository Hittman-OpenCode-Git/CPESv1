## 8. Methodology

- **Analyze item extraction:** QuestionID-anchored block parsing from all 5 pack files using boundary-aware extraction (QuestionID → next QuestionID window)
- **Apply+judgment extraction:** Same method, filtered by CognitiveLevel and keyword regex `/should|recommend|best\b|most\s+appropriate|evaluate\b|assess\b|which\s+of\s+the\s+following\s+would\s+be\s+most/i`
- **Candidate assessment:** Manual review of stem, choices, and explanation for each item
- **Evaluate criteria per mission brief:**
  - Professional judgment between competing alternatives
  - Trade-off analysis (cost vs. quality, risk vs. return)
  - Risk assessment and prioritization
  - Control evaluation and recommendation
  - Decision prioritization under constraints
  - Assessing which method/approach is BEST for a given scenario
  - Evaluating the appropriateness of a control or policy
  - Making a recommendation based on analysis of multiple factors
- **Anti-criteria applied:**
  - Pure calculation (Apply)
  - Definition recall (Remember/Understand)
  - Single-concept analysis without judgment (Analyze)
  - Formula-derived answers ("Calculate X and pick the right number")

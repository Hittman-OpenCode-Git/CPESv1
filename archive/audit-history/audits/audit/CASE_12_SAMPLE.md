### Dimension Definitions

1. **Structural/Metadata (2 pts)** — Governance fields present (question_state, pack_state, pedagogical_cluster, question_tier, question_status)? Required fields populated? No malformed data?
2. **Citation Accuracy (2 pts)** — ASC references match the topic domain? Regulatory citations accurate? No citation gaps for standards-dependent items?
3. **Calculations (2 pts)** — Numerical answers correct? Formulas correctly applied? No arithmetic errors? CalculationRequired flag matches content?
4. **Explanation Quality (2 pts)** — Explanations match the item type? Wrong-answer feedback addresses specific misconceptions? No circular or tautological explanations?
5. **Cognitive Level (2 pts)** — Item difficulty matches CognitiveLevel tag? Level appropriate for CMA Part 1? No Remember-level items masquerading as Apply/Evaluate?
6. **Business Realism (2 pts)** — Scenario plausible and coherent? Numbers realistic? No absurd premises or impossible data?
7. **DL-010 Check (pass/fail)** — Per-distractor explanations assigned to correct slots? No misassigned explanation content?

### Key Findings

1. **Structural (Dimension 1): 0/2 across ALL 75 cases** — Known systemic gap. Zero governance fields (question_state, pack_state, pedagogical_cluster) at case or item level. This is a bulk metadata operation, not a content quality issue.
2. **Citations (Dimension 2): Mixed** — Cases with ASC references (CBQ-A1, CBQ4-D3) score well. CBQ5-E3 flags ASC 450 used for Data Privacy Frameworks (mismatch). Cases without ASC references score 1/2 (no citations to verify).
3. **Calculations (Dimension 3): Strong** — Numerical items have correct answers and appropriate formulas. CBQ5-A2 and CBQ3-C2 had partial scores due to formula-structure issues.
4. **Explanations (Dimension 4): 2/2 across all 12** — Explanations match item types, provide specific feedback.
5. **Cognitive Level (Dimension 5): 2/2 across all 12** — Levels match content complexity. No mis-tagged items.
6. **Business Realism (Dimension 6): 2/2 across all 12** — Scenarios coherent, numbers realistic.
7. **DL-010: N/A** — Case items use single `Explanation` field, not per-distractor `ExplanationWrong*` slots. DL-010 cannot manifest in case items.

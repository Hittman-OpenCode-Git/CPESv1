## Classification Standard

Per Bloom's Revised Taxonomy, **Evaluate** requires the candidate to:
- Make **judgments** based on criteria and standards
- **Prioritize** between competing alternatives
- Assess **tradeoffs** (cost vs benefit, risk vs reward)
- Formulate **recommendations** supported by evidence

**Evaluate ≠ Analyze:** Analysis breaks down information and identifies patterns/diagnoses. Evaluation goes further — it requires the candidate to render a judgment or choose between defensible alternatives.

4. **Independent classification board verified:** The distinction between Analyze (diagnose/interpret) and Evaluate (judge/recommend) was explicitly checked for CC-065.

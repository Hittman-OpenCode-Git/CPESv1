## Decision

### AUTHORIZE COHORT C WITH CONDITIONS

**Rationale:** The pipeline is proven safe, quality-first, and governance-compliant. The self-detected-and-corrected defect demonstrates operational maturity. Throughput is bounded by template pool quality, not tool capability.

### Cohort C Conditions

1. **Target Sections C and D for Evaluate candidates.** Section F (Technology & Analytics) items are 83% definition-recall — forcing Evaluate upgrades produces token label changes, not cognitive depth. Sections C (Performance Management) and D (Cost Management) have judgment/analysis-rich scenarios that naturally support Evaluate-level content.

2. **Integrate Difficulty cross-validation.** Difficulty labels must be re-evaluated when CognitiveLevel changes. No separate calibration wave — cross-check at upgrade time.

3. **Remediate Pack C Section F corruption.** 15 unparseable items (missing commas) and 42 XXXMARKER artifacts must be cleaned before Cohort C certification operations touch Pack C.

4. **Continue Classification Board review.** Every Evaluate upgrade must pass independent verification. Maintain 0% false-upgrade rate.

5. **Scope Cohort C ≤50 items, ≥50% at Evaluate.** Prioritize depth over breadth. Target: 25+ Evaluate items with verified classification integrity.

### What Was NOT Authorized

- **BROAD PILOT EXPANSION** — Premature. The pipeline has proven safety but content inventory depth is insufficient for broad learner deployment.
- **HOLD** — Unnecessary. The pipeline is functioning under real authoring conditions.

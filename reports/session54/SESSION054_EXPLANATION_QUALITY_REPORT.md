## Remediation Pattern

Each thin explanation must be expanded to include:

1. **Governing standard** (e.g., "Under ASC 606," "Per COSO 2013 Principle 12,")
2. **Solution steps** (formula with substituted values for calculations; reasoning chain for conceptual items)
3. **Business interpretation** (what the result means in the case context)

**Target:** Minimum 150 characters per explanation. For items at Apply level or above, explanations should exceed 200 characters to qualify as substantive.

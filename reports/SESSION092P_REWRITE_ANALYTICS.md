## 7. Key Quantitative Findings

### Finding 1: Evaluate Overproduction
- Evaluate grew +72 (48.3% increase) vs. Analyze +54 (26.2% increase)
- Ratio: 1.33 Evaluate created per 1.0 Analyze
- CAQS target ratio: 0.6 Evaluate per 1.0 Analyze (15%/25%)
- **Risk:** Evaluate pool is outperforming Analyze pool growth, creating a skew away from CAQS targets.

### Finding 2: Campaign Concentration Risk
- 59.5% of all HO gains (75/126 items) came from a single section: Pack D Section B
- Pack A Section A contributed 14/126 (11.1%)
- Remaining ~37 items scattered across all other sections
- **Risk:** Modernization quality is benchmarked primarily on two sections. Generalizability of rewrite methodology to other domains (especially Sections A, C, D, F) is unproven.

### Finding 3: Certified-Pool Modernization Works
- 127 of 481 HO items are certified — all post-modernization
- Zero DL-008 regressions across all campaigns
- Zero DL-030 (wrong answer) introductions during rewrite
- **Finding:** The governance guard (Rules 1-9) effectively prevented certification-defect introduction during content rewrites.

### Finding 4: S380 Misclassification Rate
- 50% of sampled Evaluate-labeled items are genuinely Evaluate
- 39.4% are Apply-disguised-as-Evaluate
- 10.6% are Analyze-disguised-as-Evaluate
- **Projected Impact:** If the 50% rate holds across the pool, ~111 of 221 Evaluate items may be misclassified Apply or Analyze.

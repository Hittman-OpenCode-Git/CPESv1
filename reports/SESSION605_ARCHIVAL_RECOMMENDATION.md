## 3. Counter-Argument

### 3.1 What would be lost by archiving?

- **Visibility of GAP-3:** The ownership acceptance gap (0/6 acceptances, 3 missed deadlines) would lose its monitoring cadence. However, GAP-3 is a Governance Board action item, not a 600-series monitoring dependency. The S725 Framework §5.3 succession plan auto-triggers at S728 T0 regardless of 600-series archival status. The backstop is in place.

- **Early warning of binding constraint shift:** If the 800-series certification pipeline suddenly bottlenecked on drafting capacity, the monitoring framework would detect it. However, the trigger conditions for this (backlog > 50 items at steady state, cert velocity < 10 items/session) are objectively observable without active monitoring. The events that trigger un-archival (see §4) are the same events the monitoring framework would detect — they do not require a monitoring framework to exist.

- **Institutional memory:** The charter, trigger registry, and governance record are valuable IP. Archival preserves these artifacts in full (see §4).

- **Governance ceremony continuity:** The S600-S604 sequence is the only instance of a reserve lane going through the full charter→readiness→dormancy→certification→monitoring lifecycle. Archiving sets a precedent that dormant lanes are eventually archived rather than maintained indefinitely. This is a feature, not a bug — it prevents governance debt accumulation.

### 3.2 What risk does archiving create?

- **Accidental activation without monitoring:** Without the monitoring framework's drift detection, an unauthorized 600-series session could theoretically occur. However, the governance guard (Rule 2 BLOCK, Rule 5 BLOCK) operates at the file-modification layer, independent of the 600-series monitoring framework. The S600 Charter §3.1/§3.2/§3.3 MAY NOT prohibitions are documented and cross-referenced. The 7 prohibited activation paths in the S602 Trigger Registry remain in effect. The S603 Closure Board's activation freeze is a portfolio-level determination, not a 600-series artifact. Archival does not dissolve the freeze.

- **Trigger event missed:** If a trigger condition flips (e.g., modernization backlog exceeds 50+ items) and no one is monitoring, the 600-Series stays archived when it should be reviewed. Mitigation: (a) the trigger events are objectively observable by active lanes (300-series measures throughput, 800-series sees its own backlog), (b) the annual stewardship review at 2027-07-26 is a hard backstop, (c) the S725 succession plan auto-triggers at S728 T0 if GAP-3 persists.

This is more efficient — the Rapid Pulse result is predetermined (0/24 triggers unchanged, economic case worse) — and the monitoring pulse would produce zero new information.

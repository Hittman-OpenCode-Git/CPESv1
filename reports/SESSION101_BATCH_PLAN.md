## 5. Difficulty Calibration Notes

- DifficultyScore of `4` is the template default and almost always inappropriate for items at Remember/Understand/Apply. Recalibrate:
  - Remember → Easy(1)
  - Understand → Moderate-Easy(2) or Moderate(3)
  - Apply → Moderate(3) or Difficult(4) for multi-step
  - Analyze → Difficult(4)
  - Evaluate → Difficult(4) or Very Difficult(5)
- Items with DifficultyScore=1 but CognitiveLevel=Analyze (DD-031) — keep difficulty at Easy(1), fix COG only.
- Items with DifficultyScore=5 but COG→Remember (EC-030) — this is the DL-031 difficulty inflation defect.

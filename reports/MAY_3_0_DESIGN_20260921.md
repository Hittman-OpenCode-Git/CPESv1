## 3. Track A — Adaptive Selection Specification

### 3.1 Purpose

Replace the static `selectWithDifficultyDistribution()` (app/app.js:2748, called from app/app.js:2653) with a May-driven adaptive selection engine that consumes `MayLearnerState.getAdaptivePracticeMix()` (app/may/may-learner-state.js:778) and learner-intelligence signals (`getLearnerIntelligence()`, `getTopicProgress()`) to bias item delivery toward weak-LOS recovery while maintaining CAQS §6.1 distribution integrity.

**Current state:** `selectWithDifficultyDistribution` (app/app.js:2748–2792+) targets a per-section difficulty distribution (`distribution` parameter) using a tier-fill order (unseen Tier 1→2→3, then seen). It has **no learner-profile awareness** — the `distribution` parameter is set statically per part, not per learner. The May adaptive recommender (app/may/may-adaptive-recommender.js) generates coaching recommendations but does **not** feed back into item selection (confirmed at `reports/MAY018_DECISION_DISTRIBUTION.md` line 179: "router selects mode by action mapping — it does not consume the decision engine's `nextAction.coachingMode`").

### 3.2 Pre-Specified CAQS §6.1 ±5% Floor

**Source:** `knowledge/CAQS_v1.0.md` §6.1 (lines 417–429; target table at lines 423–427):

| Difficulty | Score | Target % | ±5% Floor | ±5% Ceiling |
|------------|-------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Easy | 1 | 15% | 10% | 20% |
| Moderate-Easy | 2 | 20% | 15% | 25% |
| Moderate | 3 | 30% | 25% | 35% |
| Difficult | 4 | 25% | 20% | 30% |
| Very Difficult | 5 | 10% | 5% | 15% |

**Board ruling (Q1 — cumulative gate, static per-session parameter retained):**
- The ±5% floor is a **cumulative 30-day delivery aggregate** gate, not a per-session constraint. The static per-session `distribution` parameter in `selectWithDifficultyDistribution` (app/app.js:2748) is **retained** — it is not replaced.
- The selection algorithm reads learner-profile signals from `may-learner-state.js:778` `getAdaptivePracticeMix()` to bias the `distribution` parameter at session-start (e.g., widening the Low bin for weak-LOS recovery), but the final selection remains constrained by the static tier-fill order (app/app.js:2766–2769: unseen Tier 1→2→3, then seen).
- The CAQS §6.1 ±5% floor is enforced at Tend via T1 telemetry: `MayTelemetry.trackDiversity()` cumulative histogram across all adaptive sessions must show each difficulty tier within ±5pp of target. `q.Difficulty` (app/app.js:2758) is the source field for tier bin assignment.

**Hard-stop citation:** HS-3 (§10.1: "Delivery diversity within CAQS §6.1 ±5% per tier"). Violation = hard stop, defect filing, board escalation.

### 3.3 T1 ≥3:1 Kill-Switch

**Source:** `reports/MAY_PHASE_2_REPLAN_20260920.md` §11.2 T1 (line 336):

> T1: Recommendation-acceptance rate + delivery-diversity ratio (top-20%/bottom-20% items, alert ≥3:1) — closes D1 soft-archiving drift [HIGH]

**Board ruling (Q2 — hard revert, sticky till Tend):**
- **Metric:** Delivery-diversity ratio = (acceptance/adoption rate on top-20% of items by DifficultyScore-weighted topic readiness) / (acceptance/adoption rate on bottom-20%). Threshold: ≥3:1 triggers kill-switch.
- **Action:** Hard revert to static `selectWithDifficultyDistribution` (app/app.js:2748) — May's adaptive bias is disabled, session selection reverts to the certified-pool static distribution. `MayTelemetry.trackSelectionMode()` emits a kill-switch event.
- **Engagement:** Kill-switch is **sticky** — remains active until the Tend validation pass confirms the ratio has returned below 3:1 AND CAQS §6.1 ±5% floor is restored. No automatic self-re-enable. Re-enable requires Tend re-validation + board acknowledgment.
- **HS-3 guard:** The kill-switch is the enforcement mechanism for HS-3. If the adaptive path cannot hold within ±5% of CAQS §6.1 targets for 3 consecutive Tend checks, the kill-switch fires.

## 4. Track B — Guided Self-Score Specification

### 4.1 Purpose

Introduce a guided self-scoring workflow for case-study responses (Part 2). The learner completes a case question, submits their written answer, and May presents a **score-authority-neutral, EV3-cited rubric** — a structured checklist of evaluation criteria drawn from CAQS_v1.0.md — that the learner applies to their own work. May never assigns the score; the learner records their self-score. May may surface what a "proficiency demonstration" would look like at each rubric level, but must not state "you scored X."

**Board ruling (F4):** "authority-neutral" → "score-authority-neutral, EV3-cited." The example criterion must reference CMA Part 2, not Part 1.

### 4.2 No-Score-Authority Constraint (CAQS §1.4)

**Source:** `knowledge/CAQS_v1.0.md` §1.4 (lines 40–48; Runtime AI rule at lines 42–44):

> **Runtime AI (learner-facing):** Never determines correctness, scoring, formulas, or blueprint mapping. Scoring authority lives with the application. Explanation and synthesis authority lives with the runtime AI.

**Track B compliance:**
- May presents the rubric criteria (e.g., "Identify the correct CMA Part 2 CSO LOS from the IMA blueprint"). The learner selects which criteria are met.
- May may echo the criterion description and provide a non-evaluative exemplar ("At the 'Analyze' level, a strong response decomposes the variance into price and quantity components and traces each to a CSO").
- **May never** outputs a score, grade, band, or percentage for the learner's response.
- The self-score field (`selfScore`) is written by the learner via UI input — May does not set it programmatically.

**HS-5 citation (EV3):** `knowledge/CAQS_v1.0.md` §4.3 EV3 (line 345): "EV3 — Correct answer must reference the accounting principle by name." Every rubric criterion must cite the governing accounting principle by name, per the EV3 standard. `reports/MAY_PHASE_2_1_DESIGN_20260920.md` §10.1 HS-5: "Micro-lessons / misconception tags / prose maps cite the governing authority (CAQS §4.3 EV3 standard, same as MCQ EC)."

**HS-6 citation:** No PII stored in self-score data. Self-scores are aggregated per-LOS (§10.1 HS-9) — never per learner profile. `MayLearnerState.getTopicProgress()` exposes `masteryCorrect`/`masteryCorrectCount` (app/may/may-learner-state.js:18, per `reports/MAY_2_5_TRACK1_CLOSEOUT_20260921.md` §2 line 18) but self-scores are stored separately and only aggregated.

## 5. Track C — HS-10 Enforcement

### 5.1 Purpose

Enforce the cognitive-budget cap (HS-10) using T5 telemetry signals and a 7-day circuit-breaker. When May's cumulative UI surface area grows beyond the T5-derived threshold, the circuit-breaker freezes new UI additions until board review.

**Source:** `reports/MAY_PHASE_2_REPLAN_20260920.md` §10.1 HS-10 (line 302): "May's cumulative UI surface bounded by T5 cognitive-budget signal (abandonment + completion-time deltas vs surface area; threshold to be set per open decision)." §11.2 T5 (line 340): "Cognitive-budget telemetry (session abandonment + completion-time deltas vs surface area)." `reports/MAY_PHASE_2_1_DESIGN_20260920.md` W9 spec (line 125): "Both signals — time-delta primary, abandonment secondary — with 7-day circuit-breaker. Threshold numbers deferred to +30d baseline (no invented threshold now)."

### 5.2 Enforcement Mechanism

- **T5 signal source:** `MayTelemetry.trackSurfaceArea()` (app/may/may-telemetry.js) logs cumulative UI surface area (px) of the May coaching panel + dashboard. `MayTelemetry.trackCognitiveBudget()` (app/may/may-learner-state.js) logs abandonment rate + median completion-time delta vs surface-area growth.
- **Threshold (post-30d):** Derived from the +30d T5 baseline (Q1 threshold synthetic benchmark in `reports/BOARD_REVIEW_20260921.md` §4 confirms abandonment 7.2–9.3% vs 8.0% baseline). The threshold value is set at +30d live baseline — **not invented at scope time** (per `reports/MAY_PHASE_2_REPLAN_20260920.md` §125: "Threshold numbers deferred to +30d baseline").
- **7-day circuit-breaker:** If T5 signals breach the threshold for 7 consecutive days, new May UI surface additions are frozen. Learner sessions continue uninterrupted (existing surface rendered; no new pills/modals/recommendations added). `MayTelemetry.trackCircuitBreaker()` emits a Tend alert.
- **Failure mode:** Board review within 72 hours. Threshold recalibration or circuit-breaker reset requires board authorization.

**HS-10 citation:** `reports/MAY_PHASE_2_REPLAN_20260920.md` §10.1 HS-10; AGENTS.md §13.1 drift signals (MEDIUM — app.js hash mismatch, agent self-report conflict; report per §3.1 Light-Lane response).

### 5.3 Lane Declaration (Track C)

**Light Lane** — Track C instruments T5 counters in `app/may/may-telemetry.js` and adds a circuit-breaker gate in `app/may/may-core.js`. Zero pack/case/registry/baseline writes. Zero delivery-logic changes (UI surface counting does not alter which items are delivered).

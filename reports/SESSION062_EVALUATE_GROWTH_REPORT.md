## Evaluate Quality Assessment

All 9 applied Evaluate rewrites meet the standard for genuine Evaluate-level items:

1. **Recommendation required** — Every item asks "which recommendation should X present?" not "which of the following is correct?"
2. **Competing alternatives** — All distractors represent plausible courses of action, not obviously wrong answers
3. **Professional judgment** — Correct answer requires weighing tradeoffs (risk vs cost, speed vs accuracy, prevention vs detection)
4. **Stakeholder framing** — Controller memos, CFO briefings, audit committee presentations — real decision contexts

### Example: P1-E-081 (Grandview Remediation Prioritization)

- **Evaluate dimension:** Prioritize 4 competing control deficiencies with $180K budget constraint
- **Tradeoff:** Proven fraud (vendor master file, $85K) vs material weakness (bank rec timing, $28K) vs recurring inefficiency (three-way match bypass, $42K) vs growing risk (journal entry review, $25K)
- **Correct answer requires:** Assessing that a proven-exploited vulnerability trumps auditor-classified material weakness in remediation priority
- **Distractor quality:** All 3 distractors present legitimate alternative prioritization frameworks (largest dollar exposure, cost-efficiency, budget optimization)

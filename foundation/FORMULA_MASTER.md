# Contribution Margin

### Domain

Cost Management

### Definition

Revenue remaining after variable costs have been deducted.

### Formula

Contribution Margin = Sales − Variable Costs

Contribution Margin Ratio = Contribution Margin ÷ Sales

### Variables

Sales

Variable Costs

Contribution Margin

### Purpose

Measures the amount available to cover fixed costs and generate operating income.

### When to Use

* CVP analysis
* Break-even analysis
* Target profit
* Product profitability
* Pricing decisions

### When NOT to Use

Do not substitute Gross Margin for Contribution Margin.

### Common Exam Traps

Confusing:

Contribution Margin

with

Gross Margin

### Validation Rules

Contribution Margin always excludes fixed costs.

Contribution Margin cannot exceed Sales.

Contribution Margin Ratio must be between 0 and 100%.

### Worked Example

Sales = $500,000

Variable Costs = $320,000

Contribution Margin = $180,000

Contribution Margin Ratio = 36%

# Break-even Point (Units)

### Domain

Cost Management

### Formula

Break-even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ Unit Contribution Margin

### Validation Rules

Break-even cannot be computed.

### Common Exam Traps

Using Selling Price instead of Contribution Margin.

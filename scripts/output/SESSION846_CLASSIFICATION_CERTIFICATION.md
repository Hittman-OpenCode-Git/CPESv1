## Executive Summary

**10 Pack E R-series items analyzed. All 10 are NEEDS ENHANCEMENT — none can be upgraded to Evaluate without stem rewrite.**

The R-series items have strong business scenarios (named companies, specific stakeholders, realistic control contexts), making them the best candidates in the pool for Evaluate-level upgrade. However, every item currently tests at Bloom's Apply — classification/identification — asking "which of the following describes/classifies/identifies [X]?" rather than "which recommendation should [stakeholder] present?"

The S836 pipeline upgrade path (Apply → Evaluate) is feasible but requires the most substantial per-item investment in the 800-series: stem format change (classification → recommendation), distractor rewrite (single-error → competing recommendations), and EC rewrite (identification → judgment rationale).

## Evaluate vs Analyze: Critical Distinction

The S846 analysis confirmed a critical finding: current Apply-level items classified as "Evaluate candidates" in S839 actually require an intermediate step first:

| Level | What it tests | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| **Apply** (current) | Classify/identify a concept | "Which describes the control weakness?" |
| **Analyze** (S845) | Interpret meaning, compare, decompose | "What does this variance tell the production manager?" |
| **Evaluate** (S846 target) | Judge among options, recommend | "Which restructuring best balances SoD and efficiency given 3 clerks?" |

Many of these 10 items may be better suited as Analyze upgrades first (requiring interpretation of the scenario) before attempting Evaluate (requiring recommendation among competing options).

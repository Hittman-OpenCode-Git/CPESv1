## Key Finding: Content Authoring is the Bottleneck

The S840 pilot established the pipeline at 17.5 min/item (3.4 items/hr). Running at this rate, the 32 deferred Analyze/Evaluate upgrades require ~13.8 hours of dedicated authoring session time. Calibration is fast (~1 min/item). Defect repair is surgical (~15 min/item).

The program has proven it can PLAN at scale (100 items across 3 cohorts) but the EXECUTION rate is limited by the pipeline's per-item time investment. This is a feature, not a bug — quality-first authoring is necessarily slower than template-based bulk authoring. The slower pace is justified by zero new structural defects and proper content defect detection.

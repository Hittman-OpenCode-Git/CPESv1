## 2. Subtopic Clusters (grounded in the 160-item inventory — full list in volume 10)

- **Liquidity:** current/quick/cash ratios; operating cash flow ratio; working-capital composition; CCC; window dressing; restricted cash.
- **Leverage/coverage:** D/E, D/A, TIE, DSCR, fixed-charge coverage, debt-to-EBITDA, Altman Z, lease-adjusted leverage, off-balance-sheet obligations.
- **Activity:** inventory turnover, DSO, DPO, total asset turnover, fixed asset turnover, working-capital turnover, receivables aging.
- **Profitability:** gross/operating/net margins, EBITDA margin, segment margin, ROCE/ROIC, EVA, MVA, quality-of-income ratio.
- **Market:** P/E, PEG, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA, dividend yield, TSR.
- **Decomposition:** 3-factor and 5-factor DuPont; margin-vs-turnover diagnosis.
- **Earnings quality:** accruals vs cash flow, channel stuffing, bill-and-hold, cookie-jar reserves, big-bath, M-Score red flags, non-GAAP adjustments (Reg G), deferred revenue as leading indicator.
- **Special topics:** VIE/ASC 810, ASC 830 translation, LIFO reserve, ASC 280 segments, capitalized interest, fiscal-year-end comparability, industry life-cycle ratio expectations, cyclical P/E normalization.

## 7. Progression Template

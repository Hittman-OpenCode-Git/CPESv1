## 2. Subtopic Clusters (full list in volume 10)

- **Risk/return:** expected return, std dev, CV, portfolio beta, two-asset portfolio variance, CML vs SML, Jensen's alpha, Treynor, APT.
- **Cost of capital:** WACC market weights, component costs (debt after-tax, preferred, CAPM equity, DDM equity, bond-yield-plus-premium), flotation adjustments, pure-play/Hamada beta, project-specific hurdle rates, MCC/IOS.
- **Capital structure:** MM propositions, trade-off theory, pecking order, distress costs, optimal leverage, EBIT-EPS indifference, DFL.
- **Working capital:** CCC (DIO+DSO−DPO), EOQ (+quantity discounts, reorder point, safety stock), Baumol + **Miller-Orr**, credit policy, lockbox, factoring, trade credit (2/10 net 30), discount-interest and compensating-balance loans.
- **Long-term financing:** bond pricing/YTM, refunding NPV, rights offerings, warrants, convertibles, lease-vs-buy (NAL), sale-leaseback.
- **Dividends/repurchases:** Gordon growth, payout/retention, stock splits vs dividends, dates, Dutch auction, clientele effect, **bird-in-hand vs tax-preference vs MM irrelevance**.
- **Restructuring:** M&A synergy valuation, accretion/dilution, merger types, divestitures, takeover defenses, LBO debt capacity, valuation methods.
- **International:** forward premium, PPP, IRP, covered interest arbitrage, money-market hedge, transaction/translation/economic exposure, multinational NPV, netting/leading-lagging, currency options.

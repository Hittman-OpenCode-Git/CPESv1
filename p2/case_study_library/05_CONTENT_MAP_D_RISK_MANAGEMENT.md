## 2. Subtopic Clusters (full list in volume 10)

- **Framework:** COSO ERM components/20 principles (by number), governance roles, three lines of defense, ERM vs silo risk management, review-and-revision triggers.
- **Assessment:** risk category classification (strategic/operational/financial/hazard/compliance), inherent vs residual, expected loss (single and multi-outcome), risk score/heat map, likelihood × severity, quantitative vs qualitative, Delphi, horizon scanning, correlation/portfolio view, VaR interpretation, scenario analysis/stress testing.
- **Appetite:** appetite vs tolerance vs capacity; appetite statements and quantitative thresholds; KRI thresholds and escalation.
- **Response:** avoid/reduce/share(transfer)/accept; mitigation cost-benefit; deductible optimization (retention); pursue/exploit; risk owner accountability; contingency acceptance.
- **Integration:** strategy-performance linkage, emerging risk, reporting to the board, risk culture and tone at top.

## 7. Progression Template

Q1 numeric (risk score or expected loss) → Q2 numeric (residual risk after controls) → Q3 select (correct response strategy) → Q4 select (appetite vs tolerance vs capacity) → Q5 multi (which risks escalate to the board) → Q6 match (risk → category/response) or fill (appetite threshold recommendation).

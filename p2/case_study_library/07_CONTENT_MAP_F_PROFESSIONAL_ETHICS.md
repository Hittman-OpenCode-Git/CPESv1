## 2. Subtopic Clusters (full list in volume 10)

- **IMA standards:** competence (limits of expertise, CPE), confidentiality (disclosure exceptions, after employment, no personal use), integrity (conflicts of interest, gifts, refrain from discreditable conduct), credibility (fair communication, error disclosure, assumptions/limitations).
- **Resolution process:** first step (internal chain), documentation, board notification, resignation as last resort; whistleblower external reporting (SOX 806).
- **Decision model:** identify → assess → alternatives → act → reflect.
- **Fraud:** triangle (pressure/opportunity/rationalization), financial-reporting red flags, management override, earnings-management pressure, inventory misstatement.
- **Governance:** SOX 302/404(b)/409/906/301, audit committee independence and responsibilities, tone at top, governance roles.
- **FCPA:** anti-bribery, books-and-records, internal controls provisions, facilitating payments exception (narrowly construed), UK Bribery Act comparison.
- **Sustainability:** GRI vs SASB vs TCFD vs IIRC integrated reporting, climate disclosure.

## 6. Exhibit Type Preferences

`email` (the pressure/request chain — core F exhibit), `policy` (codes of conduct, accounting policies), `contract` (bonus plans, supplier agreements), `table` (reserve/entry analysis, disclosure matrices), `erp-report` (journal entries), `text` (investigation notes, restatement memos).

---

## 7. Progression Template

Q1 select (which standard/principle applies) → Q2 select (first resolution step) → Q3 multi (which parties to notify, in order) → Q4 select (final action — resign vs document) → Q5 fill (model step conclusion) or match (scenario → standard/triangle element) → Q6 select (governance/FCPA/ESG judgment).

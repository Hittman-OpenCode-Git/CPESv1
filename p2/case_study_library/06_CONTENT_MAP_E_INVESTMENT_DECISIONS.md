## 2. Subtopic Clusters (full list in volume 10)

- **Methods:** NPV (accept/reject, uneven CFs, cost-savings projects, working-capital effects, salvage/terminal flows), IRR (interpolation, acceptance rule, reinvestment assumption, multiple IRRs, limitations), payback (uniform/non-uniform, with salvage), discounted payback, PI (computation, interpretation, ranking), ARR, EAA (unequal lives), crossover rate.
- **DCF mechanics:** after-tax cash flows ((Rev−Exp)(1−t)+Dep×t), MACRS + IRC §168, depreciation recapture, after-tax salvage, opportunity costs vs sunk costs, real vs nominal discount rates, risk-adjusted discount rates, certainty equivalents, expected NPV.
- **Risk:** sensitivity (most-sensitive variable), scenario analysis, Monte Carlo, decision trees (staged), RADR vs certainty-equivalent.
- **Ranking/selection:** independent vs mutually exclusive, capital rationing (single/multi-period), PI ranking, NPV vs IRR conflicts.
- **Real options:** abandon, defer/timing, expand, flexibility/switch.
- **Post-audit:** variance analysis of forecast vs actual; audit triggers.

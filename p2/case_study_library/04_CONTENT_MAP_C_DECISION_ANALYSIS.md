## 2. Subtopic Clusters (full list in volume 10)

- **CVP:** BE units/$; target profit (pre/after-tax); MOS; DOL; multi-product WACM; shut-down point; cost-structure indifference; level-vs-seasonal production; taxes and BE.
- **Marginal analysis:** special orders (idle vs full capacity, setup costs, FX-priced); make-or-buy with opportunity cost; sell-or-process-further; keep-or-replace (sunk cost); add-or-drop with allocated costs and cannibalization; scrap-vs-rework; sell-or-lease.
- **Pricing:** cost-plus vs markup-vs-margin; target costing; life-cycle costing; skimming vs penetration; price discrimination conditions; MR=MC; elasticity and CM maximization; ROI-based pricing; bundling; yield management; game theory.
- **Relevant costing:** sunk vs opportunity vs committed vs avoidable; hidden outsourcing costs.
- **Constraints:** CM per constraint unit; two-constraint corner points; **shadow price**; throughput accounting/bottleneck; outsourcing to relax a bottleneck.
- **Uncertainty:** expected value, decision trees (staged), EVPI, EVSI/Bayes, maximax/maximin/minimax regret, sensitivity vs scenario vs Monte Carlo, newsvendor.
